Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how Los Angeles Catering Experts ("we", "us") handles information on losangelescateringexperts.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, event type, guest count and the estimate you requested, and anything you send through our forms.
  • Technical information: basic data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, which our hosting and security providers process to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

  • To provide your estimate and respond to your request.
  • To connect you with catering companies that may contact you about your event.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site and prevent spam or abuse.

Who we share it with

When you submit a quote request, we may share your name, contact details and event details with catering companies so they can contact you by phone, text or email about your event. Under California law this may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out at any time on our Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information page.

We also use service providers that process data on our behalf, such as our form processor (Formspree), our hosting and network provider (Cloudflare) and our code hosting provider. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, delete it, correct it, or stop selling or sharing it. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. To make a request, use our privacy request form.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is completely secure.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies or analytics tools to understand site traffic. If we do, we will update this policy.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
