How Los Angeles catering is priced

Most caterers quote per person, then add charges for service, staffing, rentals, delivery and taxes. The calculator above uses simple planning rates by event type so you can budget before you ask for quotes.

  • Guest count: larger groups usually lower the per-person price, and many caterers set minimums.
  • Menu and service style: drop-off trays cost less than buffets, stations or plated dinners with servers.
  • Extras: rentals, bartending, travel across LA traffic, and setup or cleanup can add to the total.

Frequently asked questions

How much does catering cost per person in Los Angeles?

It depends on the menu and service. Our calculator uses planning rates of $25 to $65 per guest depending on event type. Real quotes will differ.

Is the estimate a quote?

No. It is a rough budget figure. A caterer gives you the actual price after seeing your menu, date and location.

Do you provide the catering?

No. We are an estimator and directory. You work directly with the catering company you choose.

What should I ask a caterer?

Ask for the per-person price, the minimum order, what staffing, rentals and delivery cost, and how they handle dietary needs.
